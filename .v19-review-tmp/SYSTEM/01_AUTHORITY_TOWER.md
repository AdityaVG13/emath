# Authority Tower

Highest to lowest:

```text
1. project constitution
2. feature-scoped authority lock
3. accepted Feature Capsule
4. canonical Language Image and migration tables
5. Conformance Corpus
6. implementation and generated projections
7. generated reference/capability/agent views
8. examples and tutorials
9. ELP rationale and design records
10. wave archives and research proposals
```

A lower layer may expose a defect in a higher layer; it cannot silently override it.

An unmigrated feature may name a legacy reference anchor at level 2.
